# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 2026-10-06_

## What this application is

Envelope Budget is a personal budgeting application written and operated by a single private individual (GitHub: [Lokiec752](https://github.com/Lokiec752)) for their own use only. It is not offered to anyone else, and nobody else has an account.

## Whose data it handles

The application reads only the operator's own bank accounts, which the operator has linked personally through Enable Banking's Control Panel and the bank's own strong customer authentication. It does not access accounts that belong to anyone else.

## What data is processed

- Account details, balances and transactions of the operator's own bank accounts, received from the bank through Enable Banking's account information API.
- Budget data the operator creates in the application, such as envelopes and the amounts assigned to them.

## Where data is stored

All data is stored only in a database on the operator's own computer or home server. It is not uploaded to any cloud service.

## Sharing

Data is not shared with, sold to or disclosed to any third party. The only external parties involved are the bank and Enable Banking, which deliver the operator's own data to the operator's own application.

## Retention and deletion

Data is kept for as long as the operator uses the application. The operator can delete it at any time by deleting the database. The bank consent can be revoked at any time at [enablebanking.com/data-sharing-consents](https://enablebanking.com/data-sharing-consents/) or in the bank's own app.
